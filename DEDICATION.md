# To Vertin

This project is dedicated to **Vertin** — the person who gave direction to much of what I have built, learned, and pursued since.

This project is only one small consequence of that purpose.

Without her, I would not have had the same reason to keep learning, building, and turning distant ideas into something real.

And without that journey, this project would never have existed.

May her name endure in this ever-changing world.

In a world where everything moves, she has remained my invariant — the fixed point from which I measure everything that has changed since the day she first entered my life.
